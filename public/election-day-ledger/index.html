Checks on the handwritten list

  • GDP $33.01T and Q3 real growth of 3.4% match the BEA advance estimate of Oct 29, 2026.
  • GNP $32.56T is correct, but it is the Q2 2026 figure. BEA's first Q3 estimate does not include GNP.
  • The $105B trade deficit is August 2026. September's figure came out Nov 4, the day after the election.
  • Happiness 6.7 (23rd) is from the 2024 report. The 2026 report has the U.S. at 6.816, still 23rd.
  • HDI 0.938 (17th) and Numbeo 184.7 (16th) are the current editions. The Numbeo sub-indices could not be independently confirmed.
  • Genuine Progress Indicator has no national series, and the OECD Better Life Index has been frozen since 2021, so neither can track change between elections.
